# 3D Living Room

## What is the Living Room?

The Living Room is the 3D version of a voice room. Everyone in the room appears as an avatar sitting on a couch, in a cinema, at a game table, or on a dance floor—and voices can come from where people are in the room. It's built for watching movies together, playing D&D, listening to music, or just hanging out.

To open it:

1. Join a voice room.
2. Click the armchair icon (**Living Room**) in the room's bottom bar.

The room renders in your browser; the first load can take a few seconds. If your device struggles, you can lower graphics quality in the room settings (below).

## Top toolbar

Along the top of the room:

- **Spaces:** Living room, cinema, tabletop, board hall, meadow, and club. Clicking one moves your avatar there.
- **Watch together:** Opens Watch Together; the video plays on the room's screen.
- **Overhead view** and **First-person view:** Look at the room from above or through your own eyes.
- **Room sounds:** Ambient room audio. Only you hear this.
- **Living Room settings:** Room settings (below).
- **Fullscreen** and **Hide controls (H):** Expands to fullscreen or hides the UI buttons.

At the bottom are quick reactions (👋 😂 ❤️ 🎉 🔥 👏 😮 🍿) and **Stand up**. Reactions float briefly above your avatar.

![First-person view](/help-media/img/oda-gozunden.webp)

## Moving around

- Walk with **W A S D** or the arrow keys. Hold **Shift** or double-tap **W** to run; **Space** jumps.
- **X** sits down wherever you are.
- Click any armchair, chair, or bench to sit there.

## Living room

Armchairs face a fireplace and a large TV. If someone in the room shares their screen or the music bot is playing, the stream and track appear on the TV.

## Cinema

A theater seating up to 150 people. Take any seat; screen shares or Watch Together videos play on the big screen. In the cinema, voices sound like a normal call by default; turn on **Spatial voices** in settings if you want room acoustics. You can control playback from the **projection booth** through the door at the back.

![Cinema hall](/help-media/img/oda-sinema.webp)

## Tabletop

A round table for D&D and tabletop games. Ten people can sit at the table while others stand around it.

- Roll from the **dice tray** at the bottom (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, d100, **Adv** advantage, **Dis** disadvantage). Dice roll right onto the map on the table for everyone to see.
- You can also type expressions like `2d6+3` or commands like `/spell fireball` and `/monster goblin` in the dice box.
- Grab a miniature from the **miniature cabinet** on the west wall and place it on the map.
- **Make my character** or **My Character** in the toolbar opens your character sheet: name, class, level, Max HP, Armor Class (AC), speed, and spells. **More** holds your portrait, ability scores, spell slots, and GM screen.
- The Dungeon Master can sit in the **GM's chair** and roll behind their screen—only they see the result.

## Board hall

The room's board game is laid out life-sized on the floor, and pawns hop across it as you play. Click **Start a game** or **Play** to begin; you can walk across the board or sit on a bench and watch.

![Board hall](/help-media/img/oda-oyun-salonu.webp)

## Meadow

A small outdoor world: wander wherever you like, sit around the campfire, head back to the cabin, or press **F** on the dock to fish.

![Campfire in the meadow](/help-media/img/oda-cayir.webp)

## Club

A nightclub where the lights pulse with the music. Dance on the floor, or sit in a booth or at the bar.

- If nothing is playing, click **Start the night** at the bar to kick things off.
- **Take the decks** puts you in the DJ booth with mixing, remixes, and a sampler. Details in [Music Bot & DJ Mode](/en/help/music).
- **Lights** opens the lighting console.
- Type a song name in the box at the bottom and click **Request** to request a track from the DJ.

![Club](/help-media/img/oda-kulup.webp)

## Room settings

Under **Living Room settings**:

- **Spatial voices:** Hear people from where they are in the room. When off, everyone sounds like a normal call.
- **Room echo:** Room reverb.
- **Graphics:** Visual quality: **Auto**, **High**, **Medium**, or **Low**. Turn it down if your laptop or phone gets warm.
- **Music from the TV:** Plays the music bot's audio from the TV.

These settings affect only your screen and your ears.
