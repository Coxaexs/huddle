# Games, Activities & Events

## In-chat games

You can play quick games with friends in any text channel or direct message.

1. Click **^** (**More options**) on the right of the message box and choose **Play Game**.
2. Pick a game in the **Games** dialog:
   - **Tic-Tac-Toe (XOX):** Get three in a row on a 3×3 board.
   - **Connect Four:** Drop discs and line up four in a row.
   - **Rock Paper Scissors:** Best two out of three; picks stay hidden until both choose.
   - **Minesweeper Flags:** Find the mines; first to 26 wins.
   - **Rota:** Connect two countries by naming the countries in between.
3. The game posts as an invite card in chat with an **Open seat**. When a friend clicks **Accept**, the game begins; they can click **Decline** if they don't want to play, or you can click **Cancel** to withdraw it.

The top of the card shows whose turn it is (**Your turn.**). You can **Forfeit** mid-game or click **Play again** once it's over.

You can also play Rota solo: click **Play solo** next to Rota in the same dialog.

### Rota

Rota gives you two countries on a map (for example Cyprus and Qatar). Type the names of the countries forming the shortest land route between them and click **Guess**. Each guess is color-coded:

- **Green:** On the shortest route.
- **Yellow:** One step longer.
- **Red:** Two or more steps longer.

Dashed lines mark sea crossings. You can enter country names in English (**EN**) or Turkish (**TR**). Click **Give up** if you get stuck.

## Voice room activities

While in a voice room, click the gamepad icon (**Activities**) in the bottom bar. Everyone in the room shares the same activity together:

| Activity | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Watch Together**, **Reels Together** | Watch videos and Shorts in sync ([Watch Together](/en/help/watch-together)). |
| **Whiteboard** | Shared whiteboard: pick colors and brush sizes, draw and plan together. **Clear** wipes the board. |
| **Tier List** | Rank items together as a group (S, A, B…). |
| **Draw & Guess** | Take turns drawing a secret word while others guess. |
| **Shared Timer** | Countdown everyone sees at the same time. |
| **Initiative Tracker** | Turn order tracker for D&D combat ([D&D](/en/help/dnd#initiative-tracker)). |
| **DeepPixel** | Multiplayer property board game (also works with bots); plays life-sized in the 3D Living Room's board hall. |
| **Hand-Gesture Synth** | Play chords together by making hand gestures at your camera. |
| **Hoffle Beatbox** | Build beats together (below). |

Click **End** in the top right to close an activity, or **⌄** to minimize it back to the voice room view.

### Hoffle Beatbox

A shared drum machine and bassline sequencer everyone in the room edits together. Rows show drum hits (Kick, Snare, Hat, Open hat, Clap) and the bassline; each square is one step.

- Click a square to toggle a hit on or off. Drag a bass note up or down to change its pitch.
- **Tempo** sets the speed, **Randomize** generates a random beat, and **Clear** resets everything.
- Shape the bass sound with **Saw**/**Square**, **Cutoff**, **Resonance**, and **Env mod**.
- The pattern is shared with everyone, while **Play** and volume are local—each person listens whenever they want.

## Events

Create an event to schedule a hangout, movie night, or D&D session so members can RSVP.

1. Click **Events** at the top of the channel list.
2. Click **New event**.
3. Give the event a **name** and pick a **start** time (and optional end time).
4. Optionally link a **voice channel** and add **details**.
5. Click **Create event**.

::video etkinlik-olustur | Creating a "Friday movie night" event

![Event card](/help-media/img/etkinlik-karti.webp)

On the event card, members choose **Going**, **Maybe**, or **Can't go**, and attendees are listed right on the card. Upcoming events appear at the top of the channel list with their date. The download button on a card adds it to your calendar (`.ics`), and **Subscribe** subscribes your calendar app to all events in the server. Edit with the pencil or delete with the trash icon.
