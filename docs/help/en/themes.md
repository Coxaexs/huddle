# Themes & Y2K / MSN Features

## Changing your theme

1. Open **Settings → Appearance** and go to the **Themes** tab (in Simple mode, **Settings → Look of Hoffle**).
2. Click **Apply** on any theme card you like. Hoffle switches right away.

Themes only change your own screen—your friends see whichever theme they picked. You can also pick a theme during the initial welcome tour.

## Built-in themes

| Theme | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Cozy** | The default theme: deep purple-tinted dark background, rounded cards. |
| **Dark Academia** | Old library vibe: forest green leather, aged paper, burgundy, and gold. |
| **Amber** | Warm amber glow like fireplace light. |
| **Legacy** | Classic dark charcoal theme. |
| **Light**, **Linen** | Light themes; Linen has soft warm-linen tones. **Linen Dark** is its night version. |
| **Y2K Chat**, **Y2K Chat Dark** | 2000s instant messengers: bright blue windows and "says:" message headers. Changes the layout as well as the colors (below). |
| **Classic Winamp**, **TeamSpeak 2** | Themes that dress the whole app up like those classic programs. |
| **Cyberpunk**, **Matrix**, **Midnight** | Neon yellow and cyan; green text and digital rain; pitch-black for OLED screens. |
| **Vampire**, **Sunset**, **Zombie**, **Blocks** and more | Crimson gothic, sunset gradients, outbreak night, block-world, and other themed looks. |

In the **Customize** tab you can tweak your chosen theme's colors and details. **Create** builds a theme from scratch, **Import** loads a theme someone shared with you, and **Community** browses themes shared by others. For deeper tweaks, use [Custom CSS](/en/help/settings#custom-css).

## Layout

On the same **Appearance** page, under **Layout** above the themes, you can move the channel list to the left, right, top, or bottom; put the member list on the right or left; and move the call controls (voice buttons) to the bottom, top, or sides. The **Show** row toggles the server banner, activities, and channel topic. **Customize layout & widgets…** lets you add widgets to the screen.

## Y2K Chat theme and MSN features

The **Y2K Chat** theme turns Hoffle into a 2000s messenger: a window title bar and menu bar, an "Online / Offline" contact list on the right, "… says:" headers on messages, and a classic toolbar above the message box.

![Y2K Chat theme](/help-media/img/tema-y2k.webp)

Toolbar buttons:

- **Effects:** Gives your message colors, fonts, and effects (same as [Text Formatting](/en/help/messaging#text-effects-colors-and-fonts)).
- **Emoticons (☺):** Classic emoticons—for example `(Y)` becomes 👍 and `(L)` becomes ❤️. You can add your own too.
- **Winks:** Sends short full-screen animations that play on everyone's screen. Pick one from the list and it plays for the whole channel. Anyone who missed it can click **Play** on the wink line in chat to replay it.
- **Send Voice Clip:** Records and sends a short voice clip.
- **Handwriting:** Opens a drawing pad; whatever you write or sketch by hand sends as an image.
- **Backgrounds:** Changes the chat window's background.
- **Play Together:** Starts a game with people in the channel ([Games](/en/help/games)).
- **Send Nudge!:** Briefly shakes the window for everyone in the channel to get their attention. There's a short cooldown between nudges so it can't be spammed.

The **Y2K Today** window opens once a day and summarizes what's happening on Hoffle: who's around, chats waiting for you, upcoming events, what's playing, and a daily tip.

Nudges and winks arrive regardless of which theme you use. If you'd rather not get them, turn off **Nudges & Winks** under **Settings → Appearance → Extras → Features**—you can still manually replay a wink from its line in chat. The **Retro 2000s sound effects** toggle right next to it turns on Y2K-style notification sounds.
