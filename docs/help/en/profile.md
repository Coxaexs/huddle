# Profile, Customization & Achievements

## Opening the profile editor

Open **Settings** and click **Edit Profiles** under your name at the top left. In Simple mode, use the **Settings → Your picture & name** card.

Everything you can edit sits on the left, with a live preview of your profile card in the middle. Changes appear in the preview right away. Clicking **Cancel** discards changes; clicking **Apply** saves them.

## Preset looks

The **Browse looks** button at the top opens preset themes that restyle your name, profile card, and voice room look in one click: Sakura, Synthwave, Abyss, Toxic, Royal, Arcade, Frostbite, Inferno, Cotton Candy, Noir, and more. Click one, preview it on the right, and hit **Apply** if you like it. Afterward, you can still tweak every piece individually.

![Preset looks](/help-media/img/profil-gorunumler.webp)

## Identity tab

- **Display Name Style:** Font, effect, and colors for your name.
- **Nameplate:** The banner behind your name in the member list.
- **Avatar & Decoration:** Click **Upload** to set a profile picture. The **+** next to it opens avatar decorations: vampire collar, cat ears, bunny ears, halo, crown, flower crown, frog hat, glowing rings, and more. Your chosen decoration previews at multiple sizes on the right.
- **Theme & Banner:** Your card's accent color, banner image, and **Card Theme** (which tints the whole card in two colors).
- **Profile Effect & Frame:** Animated effects and frames that play on your profile card.
- **Voice:** The ring that lights up around your avatar while talking in voice, and the entrance effect when you join a room.

![Avatar decoration preview](/help-media/img/profil-cerceve-onizleme.webp)

Decorations and effects are visible to others. If you prefer not to see other people's effects, turn off **Profile Cosmetics & FX** under **Settings → Appearance → Extras → Features** (see [Settings](/en/help/settings#turning-features-on-and-off)).

## About, Connections, and Music

- **About:** Write a bio up to 500 characters in **About me**. You can also add up to 4 **Pride badges**; these are public, so pick only what you want to share.
- **Connections:** If you're in a server with a Discord bridge, link your Discord account so your Discord messages appear under your name here and vice versa.
- **Music:** Pin a song to your profile; it shows as "Listening to" on your card. For live Spotify scrobbling, see [Settings → Activities](/en/help/settings#activities).
- **CSS:** Add custom CSS rules to your profile card.

![About tab](/help-media/img/profil-sekme-about.webp)

## Your status

Click your name in the bottom left. In the menu:

- **Online**, **Idle**, **Do Not Disturb** (silences notifications), or **Invisible** (appears offline while you keep using everything).
- **Set status:** A short line shown under your name (like "watching a movie 🍿"). Pick when it clears: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours, today, or never.
- Shortcuts to **Edit profile** and **Settings**.

![Status menu](/help-media/img/durum-menusu.webp)

## Achievements

As you use Hoffle, you unlock achievements—sending your first message, replying to someone, spending an hour in voice, creating a server, visiting all six 3D spaces, and more. There are 93 achievements in total; some are secret.

- Click the medal icon (**Achievements**) in the top bar.
- Browse unlocked and locked achievements by category (Chatting, Dedication, Voice, Social, Community, Creator, Explorer, Secrets). Each shows what percentage of members have unlocked it.
- You can showcase up to 5 unlocked achievements on your profile (**On your profile**).

![Achievements](/help-media/img/basarimlar.webp)

If you don't want achievement popups, turn off **Achievements** under **Settings → Appearance → Extras → Features**.
