# Settings

## Opening settings

Click the gear icon (**User Settings**) next to your name in the bottom left. In Simple mode, settings open as large cards; click **Show everything** at the bottom to see every section.

Type a keyword (like "notifications" or "password") into **Search settings…** at the top left of the settings window to jump straight to a setting.

## Password & Security

![Password & Security](/help-media/img/ayar-guvenlik-hesap.webp)

- **Username:** The name you sign in with. Click **Change username** to update it.
- **Email:** Optional, used only for password reset links if you forget your password. Enter your address, click **Send code to add email**, and enter the code.
- **Password:** Enter your current password first to set a new one. Changing your password signs out all other devices.
- **Where you're signed in:** Devices currently logged into your account. If you see a device you don't recognize, sign it out and change your password.
- **Two-factor authentication:** When enabled, Hoffle asks for a code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Aegis, 1Password, Authy…) after your password, so someone who learns your password still can't get in.
- **Passkeys:** Sign in with fingerprint, face unlock, or your device PIN instead of a password. Click **Add passkey** and follow your device's prompt. When 2FA is on, a passkey can also act as your second step.
- **Your data:** **Download my data** exports your profile, messages, reactions, friends, servers, and settings as a file (up to three times per hour).
- **Delete account:** Deletes your account. Unless you delete them yourself, your messages remain in conversations under "Deleted User".

## Privacy & Safety

- **Who can start a chat with you:** **Everyone**, **Friends and people in your servers**, or **Friends only**. This applies to new DMs and group invites; people you're already talking to can keep messaging you.
- **Streamer mode:** Blurs your email and invite codes and suppresses notification popups so they don't appear on stream. Choose **Off**, **On while sharing my screen**, or **Always on**.

![Privacy settings](/help-media/img/ayar-gizlilik.webp)

## Activities

- **Share Spotify / Music Listening:** Displays the song you're listening to on your profile card. Connect Spotify to Last.fm and enter your Last.fm username here; your track updates within about 15 seconds.
- **Share games & apps:** Automatic game detection works in the desktop app. In the browser, you can type what you're up to in **What you are doing**.

Friends see this in the **Active Now** panel on the right of the Friends screen.

## Notifications

![Notification settings](/help-media/img/ayar-bildirimler.webp)

- **Desktop notifications:** For mentions, direct messages, and calls—even when the tab is closed.
- **Quiet hours:** During hours you choose, notifications won't pop up, play sounds, or buzz your phone. Calls still ring.
- **Keyword alerts:** Whenever someone writes one of your keywords in a server, you get notified as if you were mentioned (useful for a nickname or your favorite game).
- **Phone notifications (ntfy):** Get notifications on your phone even when the app is closed. Install the **ntfy** app on your phone, click **Make me a topic** for a private topic (or paste your own), then click **Save & test**.
- **Weekly digest:** Every Sunday, a summary of top-reacted messages, upcoming events, and new members across your servers: **Off**, **To my notes**, or **Notes + email**.

To customize notifications for a single channel or server, right-click the channel or server name (see [Channel settings](/en/help/servers#channel-settings)).

## Text-to-Speech (TTS) & Live Captions

- **Read /tts messages aloud on this device:** Speaks `/tts` messages sent in channels on this device.
- **Your voice:** The voice used for your own `/tts` messages, with **Speed**, **Pitch**, and **Effect** (natural or robot). Click **Preview** to listen.
- **Live captions:** The speech recognizer that transcribes speech during calls. **Duyu** is the server's Turkish recognizer; **Browser** uses your browser's built-in recognizer (Chrome, Edge, and Safari).

![TTS settings](/help-media/img/ayar-tts.webp)

## Accessibility

- **Chat font size:** Between 12 and 24 pixels.
- **Always underline links:** Makes links in messages easier to spot.
- **Display name styles:** Toggles colored and styled names for other users.
- **Animate name effects:** Play animated name styles on hover only, always, or never.
- **Interface motion:** Smooth scrolling and subtle animations. Turn off if motion bothers you.

The **preview** at the top of the window shows how your changes will look.

![Accessibility settings](/help-media/img/ayar-erisilebilirlik.webp)

## Turning features on and off

Under **Appearance → Extras**, you'll find **Features**. Turning off features you don't use removes their buttons, menu items, and commands **on your screen only**—other people still see theirs:

- **D&D & Tabletop**, **Mini-Games**, **Music & Jam Synth**, **3D Living Room & Cinema**
- **Profile Cosmetics & FX:** Profile effects, avatar frames, and decorations.
- **Achievements:** Achievement toasts and the achievements button.
- **Nudges & Winks:** Nudges and full-screen winks sent by others. Even when off, you can still replay a wink from its line in chat.

The same section has toggles for **Desktop buddy** (a small buddy matching your theme), **Button click particles**, **Profile & background particles**, and **Retro 2000s sound effects**.

![Feature toggles](/help-media/img/ayar-ozellikler.webp)

## Custom CSS

In **Custom CSS**, you can restyle Hoffle with your own CSS rules. Changes apply only on your screen. **Quick Presets** includes ready-made styles (Frosted Glass, Retro Dashboard, Neon Matrix, Classic Dark, Pitch Black, Pastel Dream). Write your CSS and click **Save & Apply**; toggle **Enable Client UI Custom CSS** anytime to switch it off.

![Custom CSS](/help-media/img/ayar-ozel-css.webp)

## Other sections

- **Voice & Video:** Microphone, speakers, noise suppression, and push-to-talk. Details in [Voice & Video settings](/en/help/voice-chat#voice-video-settings).
- **Appearance:** Themes, layout, and interface mode (Simple/Full). See [Simple mode and Full mode](/en/help/getting-started#simple-mode-and-full-mode).
- **Language:** Hoffle's interface language. Picks your browser language by default. Languages marked "Partial" show English where translations aren't finished yet.
- **Dice & Tabletop:** 3D dice appearance. See [Customizing your 3D dice](/en/help/dnd#customizing-your-3d-dice).
- **Feedback & Bugs:** Send a suggestion or bug report directly to Hoffle's developers and track its status under **My Requests**.
- **Guide:** Reopens the welcome tour.
- **Sign Out:** Signs out on this device.
