# Friends & Direct Messages

## Friends screen

Click the **h** button at the very top of the server rail. On the left are **Friends** and your **Direct Messages** list; in the middle is your friends list. The tabs at the top are:

- **Online:** Friends who are currently online.
- **All:** All of your friends.
- **Pending:** Incoming and outgoing friend requests.
- **Blocked:** People you've blocked.
- **Add Friend:** Send a new friend request.

The **Active Now** panel on the right shows when a friend joins a voice room, launches a game, or listens to music.

## Adding a friend

1. Click **h**, then click **Add Friend** at the top.
2. Enter your friend's **username** (not their display name; the username starts with `@` on their profile card).
3. Click **Send Friend Request**.

Your request appears under the **Pending** tab as an *Outgoing friend request*. Click **×** next to it if you change your mind.

You can also right-click someone you share a server with and send a friend request from their menu.

## Accepting a friend request

When someone sends you a request, a red badge appears next to **Friends**.

1. Click **h** and open the **Pending** tab.
2. Under *Incoming friend request*, click **✓ (Accept)** next to their name. **× (Ignore)** declines the request, and the shield icon (**Block User**) blocks them.

## Direct messages (DM)

To chat one-on-one with a friend:

- Click the speech bubble (**Message**) next to their name in your friends list, or
- Right-click their name anywhere and choose **Message**.

Conversations you open stay in the **Direct Messages** list on the left. Hovering over a conversation reveals buttons to **pin it to the top** or **close it from the list** (messages aren't deleted). Friends who send you new messages also appear as small round avatars on the server rail.

![Direct message](/help-media/img/dm-sohbet.webp)

DMs work just like channels: replies, reactions, GIFs, files, polls, and text effects all work here too.

## Voice and video calls

In a DM's top bar there are two buttons:

- **Start Call:** Voice call. Once the other person accepts, you're talking.
- **Video Call:** Call with camera on.

During a call, you control your microphone, camera, and screen share the same way as in voice rooms. Details in [Voice Chat](/en/help/voice-chat).

## Group DMs

To talk with a few friends without creating a server, open a group DM:

1. Click **+** (**Create DM or group**) next to the **Direct Messages** header.
2. Optionally type a **group name**.
3. Check the friends you want to add (up to 10 people including you).
4. Click **Create Group DM**.

![Create group DM dialog](/help-media/img/grup-dm.webp)

## Note to self

The note icon (**Note to self**) next to the **Direct Messages** header opens a private chat only you can see. Drop links, to-dos, or drafts here.

## Blocking

Right-click someone's name and choose **Block**. Blocked users can't send you direct messages or friend requests, and their messages in shared servers are hidden on your screen. To unblock someone, open **h → Blocked**.

You can also restrict who can message you under **Settings → Privacy & Safety**.
