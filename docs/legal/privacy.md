# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 October 2026_

This policy explains what Hoffle at **chat.hoffle.online** and the Hoffle apps collect, why, and what you can do about it. Hoffle is run by Abdullah Türk, who is responsible for your data (the "controller"). Contact: [info@hoffle.online](mailto:info@hoffle.online).

**The short version:** we collect what's needed to run a chat app, nothing more. No ads, no tracking, no selling data, no analytics SDKs.

## What we collect

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Account:** username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash), and optionally your email | To sign you in and, if you added an email, to send password-reset codes |
| **Profile:** avatar, banner, bio, status and other profile choices | To show your profile to others |
| **What you post:** messages, reactions, files, images, voice messages, polls, events | To deliver it to the people you sent it to and keep your history |
| **Voice and video:** your microphone, camera and screen share while you're in a call | Streamed live to the others in the call through our own voice server. Not recorded unless someone in the channel starts a recording, which everyone in the channel is shown |
| **Social:** servers you joined, friends, blocks, DMs, read positions | To make the app work |
| **Reports and moderation:** reports you send or that are made about your messages | To keep Hoffle safe |
| **Technical:** IP address, device and browser type, and server logs | Security, preventing spam and abuse, and fixing errors. A hashed IP address is kept with new accounts to limit fake sign-ups |
| **Push tokens:** a device token if you allow notifications | To send you notifications |

We don't collect your contacts, location, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Microphone, camera and photos

The app asks for the microphone for voice channels, calls and voice messages; the camera for video and taking photos to send; and your photos only when you pick a photo to send. You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.

## Services that help run Hoffle

We share only what each one needs:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting network, security, and the "are you human" check on sign-up.
- **Resend:** sends password-reset and verification emails (your email address and the message).
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Android) and **Apple Push Notification service** (iPhone): deliver notifications.
- **Google Gemini:** only when you use an AI feature (asking a question or summarising a channel). The text of your question and the messages it's about are sent to Google to produce the answer.
- **Speech services:** only if you turn on live captions or text-to-speech. The audio or text is sent to a speech provider to be converted. Captions can also be made by your own browser.
- **Klipy:** your GIF searches, when you search for GIFs.
- **Link previews:** when you post a link, our server fetches that page to show a preview. The site sees our server, not you.

Voice and video run on our own servers. We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising.

## How long we keep things

- Your account and messages: until you delete them or your account.
- Large shared files: deleted after 12 hours.
- Server logs: kept briefly, normally a few weeks.
- Backups: overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted data disappears from them within about 30 days.

## Deleting your account

Go to **Settings → Account & Password → Delete account**. That removes your account and profile, and you can choose to delete your messages too. You can also email us to ask.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to or limit how we use it, and complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). Email [info@hoffle.online](mailto:info@hoffle.online) and we'll answer within 30 days. We process data to provide the service you asked for (the contract), for our legitimate interest in keeping Hoffle secure, and with your consent for optional things like notifications.

## Children

Hoffle is not for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app.
