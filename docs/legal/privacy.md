# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

This policy explains what Hoffle at **chat.hoffle.online** and the Hoffle apps collect, why, and what you can do about it. Hoffle is run by Abdullah Türk, who is responsible for your data (the "controller"). Contact: [info@hoffle.online](mailto:info@hoffle.online).

**The short version:** we collect what's needed to run a chat app, nothing more. No ads, no tracking, no selling data, no analytics SDKs.

## What we collect

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| **Account:** username, display name, password (stored only as a secure hash), and optionally your email | To sign you in and, if you added an email, to send password-reset codes |
| **Profile:** avatar, banner, bio, status and other profile choices | To show your profile to others |
| **What you post:** messages, reactions, files, images, voice messages, polls, events | To deliver it to the people you sent it to and keep your history |
| **Voice and video:** your microphone, camera and screen share while you're in a call | Streamed live to the others in the call through our own voice server. Not recorded unless someone in the channel starts a recording, which everyone in the channel is shown |
| **Social:** servers you joined, friends, blocks, DMs, read positions | To make the app work |
| **Reports and moderation:** reports you send or that are made about your messages, including direct messages | To keep Hoffle safe |
| **Music bot and Watch Together:** in servers that added the music bot, what you played (your name, the song and its link, when and for how long), your playlists, favourites and dislikes, and what you like in Reels Together | To run the queue, show stats and Wrapped, and pick what comes next |
| **Activity status (desktop app):** the game or app you are using and what Spotify is playing | To show it on your profile. You can turn this off in Settings |
| **Your age answer:** the time you told us you are 18 or older, if you use an AI feature | AI features are for people 18 and over |
| **Bots you make:** their name, description and settings, and a scrambled copy of their token | To run the Developer Portal |
| **Technical:** IP address, device and browser type, and server logs | Security, preventing spam and abuse, and fixing errors. A hashed IP address is kept with new accounts to limit fake sign-ups |
| **Push tokens:** a device token if you allow notifications | To send you notifications |

We don't collect your contacts, location, or advertising identifiers, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Who can read your messages

Messages are not end-to-end encrypted. The people in a conversation can read it, and server owners and moderators can read and remove messages in their servers. The person who runs Hoffle can search and read messages, including direct messages. We do this only to deal with reports, abuse, security problems or legal orders. When you report a direct message, we read that message.

## Microphone, camera and photos

The app asks for the microphone for voice channels, calls and voice messages; the camera for video and taking photos to send; and your photos only when you pick a photo to send. You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.

## Services that help run Hoffle

We share only what each one needs:

- **Cloudflare:** hosting network, security, email routing, and the "are you human" check on sign-up.
- **Oracle Cloud (Frankfurt, Germany):** runs our standby server and one of our voice servers, and keeps a copy of our backups.
- **Resend:** sends password-reset and verification emails (your email address and the message).
- **Google Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Android) and **Apple Push Notification service** (iPhone): deliver notifications.
- **Google Gemini:** only when you use an AI feature (asking a question, summarising a channel, translating a message or a call recap). The text of your question and the messages or captions it's about are sent to Google to produce the answer.
- **Duyu (speech service):** only if you turn on live captions or text-to-speech. The audio or text is sent to Duyu to be converted. Captions can also be made by your own browser.
- **Klipy:** your GIF searches, when you search for GIFs.
- **Discord:** only in servers that set up the Discord bridge. Messages, names, profile pictures, files and reactions in the paired channels are copied to Discord and back, and voice is carried between paired voice rooms. Discord handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.
- **Music and video sources:** when the music bot or Watch Together plays something, our server sends the search or link to YouTube or the site it comes from, and the song title to lrclib and Musixmatch (through RapidAPI) for lyrics. Those sites see our server, not you.
- **Last.fm:** only if you add a Last.fm username to your profile. We ask Last.fm what you are listening to.
- **Google Fonts:** only when you use the Winamp theme. Your browser loads its fonts from Google, which sees your IP address.
- **ntfy:** only if you add an ntfy address for phone notifications. Your notifications are sent there.
- **Bots made by other people:** a bot added to a server is run by whoever made it, not by us. It can see what that server allowed it to see, for example the messages in channels it can read. What it does with that is up to its maker. It can't see your direct messages. If you open a bot's activity, the bot learns your name and picture, and its page loads from its own server. Audio a bot plays also comes from its server. Those servers see your IP address.
- **Link previews:** when you post a link, our server fetches that page to show a preview. The site sees our server, not you.

Voice and video run on servers we run ourselves, including on Oracle Cloud. Some of the companies above are outside the EU, mainly in the US, so your data can go there. We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising.

## How long we keep things

- Your account and messages: until you delete them or your account.
- Large shared files: deleted after 12 hours.
- Music bot records (what you played, playlists, favourites, Reels likes): until you ask us to delete them.
- Content removed under a legal order: hidden from everyone and kept for as long as the law requires, which is at least 6 months for terrorist content.
- Server logs: normally up to about 6 weeks. Voice server logs can be kept longer.
- Backups: overwritten on a rolling schedule, so deleted data disappears from them within about 30 days.

## Deleting your account

Go to **Settings → Account & Password → Delete account**. That removes your account and profile, and you can choose to delete your messages too. You can also email us to ask. Deleting your account does not remove your music bot records, so email us and we'll delete those too.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, object to or limit how we use it, and complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens). To get a copy of your data yourself, go to **Settings → Account & Password → Your data → Download my data**. That download does not include your music bot records. For those and anything else, email [info@hoffle.online](mailto:info@hoffle.online) and we'll answer within 30 days. We process data to provide the service you asked for (the contract), for our legitimate interest in keeping Hoffle secure, and with your consent for optional things like notifications.

## Children

Hoffle is not for children under 16. If you think a child under 16 has an account, email us and we'll delete it. AI features are for people 18 and over. We ask before your first use, and we don't check the answer.

## Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the app.
